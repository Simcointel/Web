Privacy Policy for SimcoIntel Extension
=========================================

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Data Collection: None. This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data Storage: The extension stores only:
• Your selected realm number (0 or 1)
• Company data synced from SimCompanies pages (company name, buildings, workers, admin overhead)

This data is stored exclusively in your browser's local chrome.storage.local and never sent to any server.

Network Requests: The extension only makes GET requests to:
• raw.githubusercontent.com/SimcoIntel/Data — public market data
• simcointel.github.io/Web — your own dashboard

No POST requests. No authentication. No tracking.

Contact: For privacy concerns, open an issue on the GitHub repository.

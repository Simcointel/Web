Extension Store Submission Checklist
=====================================

Chrome Web Store
----------------
1. Go to https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole
2. Pay one-time $5 developer registration fee
3. Click "New Item" and upload: extension/simcointel-extension.zip
4. Fill in:
   - Name: SimcoIntel — SimCo Market Data
   - Short description: (from chrome-listing.txt)
   - Long description: (from chrome-listing.txt)
   - Screenshots: (take 1280x800 screenshots of popup + overlay)
   - Icon: icons/icon128.png
   - Category: Productivity
   - Language: English
5. Privacy: (from privacy-policy.txt)
6. Submit for review

Firefox Add-ons
---------------
1. Go to https://addons.mozilla.org/developers/
2. Sign in with Firefox Account
3. Click "Submit a New Add-on" → "On your own"
4. Upload: extension/simcointel-extension.zip
5. Fill in:
   - Name: SimcoIntel — SimCo Market Data
   - Description: (from firefox-listing.txt)
   - Icon: icons/icon128.png
   - Categories: Productivity
6. Privacy: (from privacy-policy.txt)
7. Submit for review

Note: Firefox accepts unlisted distribution for testing first.

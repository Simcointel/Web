Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

SimcoIntel ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SimcoIntel browser extension and website. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Browser Storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • Selected realm number (0 or 1)
  • Company data synced from SimCompanies (company ID, name, level, rank, value, buildings, workers, admin overhead)
  • Pairing code (UUID generated locally)
  • Price alert history (item name, quality, price, discount %, timestamp)

All data stays in your browser. We do not operate servers. No data is transmitted to us.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, address)
  • No browsing history
  • No cookies
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No account registration

Network Requests

The extension makes GET-only requests to:

  • raw.githubusercontent.com/SimcoIntel/Data — public market data (margins, retail, dashboard)
  • www.simcompanies.com — reads page content you are already viewing (no POST, no form submission, no automation)

We never send data to SimCompanies servers. We never make POST, PUT, or DELETE requests.

Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Retention

All data is stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the extension permanently deletes all stored information.

Children's Privacy

Our extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For questions, open an issue at github.com/SimcoIntel/Web.
